Devlog

One week, three islands, eight axe swings

NECRO was a one-week remake study: rebuild the first three islands of a mobile idle RPG I like, with my own hero and setting, in the phone browser. On Monday the agents turned gameplay videos into a design doc and a first playable island. By Friday there were 460 commits.

On day three I told them all the islands looked depressing, so islands 2 and 3 got new looks. The axe swing alone took eight versions. One of them failed because the test dummy stood 7 units away while real enemies come within 3.4, so the ghost hand kept reaching across the gap.
