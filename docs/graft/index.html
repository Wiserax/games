Devlog

You become what you kill

GRAFT started on 14 October from Zombie Tower's leftover look test. The pitch went through four designs that same afternoon, from "You become what you kill" to "Kill the beast. Wear its body." A week later it shipped with real multiplayer: one phone hosts the match and up to 12 players join with a 5-letter code.

The friend playtest was humbling. All three friends quit around rounds 3 to 5, and a timed run showed only 29% of the first six minutes was actual fighting. One friend's phone ran it at 6 FPS, so it got an ultra-low mode. And a scary FPS drop on my Mac turned out to be Dota 2 eating 450% CPU in the background.
